Thinkspace for Windows
======================

To run it: double-click thinkspace.exe. There is nothing to install.
Keep the whole folder together; the program needs the files beside it.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
program is not signed by a company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Pasting pictures: take a screenshot with Win+Shift+S, then press Ctrl+V in
Thinkspace. You can also copy picture files in Explorer and paste them, or
drag them onto the window.

Your work is saved where you choose with Ctrl+S. An automatic copy of
unsaved work is kept in %APPDATA%\Thinkspace in case something goes wrong.
