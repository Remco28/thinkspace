**9. Overall, did the text work?** Mostly, and it's slightly worse than "fine". I matched the owner's complaint on three of the eight tasks (2, 4, 6), and my experience fits the cause he can't put a name to. **It isn't one contrast problem. Almost every piece of text is the same small size, and the text that should guide your eye is the faintest.**
- **The headings are the weakest text in the panels.** "LAYERS", "IMAGE", "PAGE" and "NEW PEN STROKE" are tiny, light grey and spaced-out capitals. "Size and rotation", "Page size" and "Edit" are small and bold, but grey, so they come out about as heavy as the field labels beside them. Your eye has nothing to grab first, so you read the right column from the top every time.

**11. The one thing I'd change:** make section headings the strongest text in the right column, and darken the grey text. Concretely:
