# Thinkspace selection label and lock tip: first-time user review

## 1. What the label says (02 and 04)
From left to right:
- **Bold text** ("t3 code 1", "awning_02") is the name of the thing I selected. It matches the highlighted row in the Layers panel and the "Name" box on the right.
- **Stacked-sheets icon + lighter text** ("t3 code", "Navy awnings") is the layer that thing sits on. The icon looks like the usual "layers" symbol, so it reads as "on layer: …". It matches the bold group headings in the Layers panel and the "Layer" dropdown.
- **Thin divider, then a padlock**, which is the lock button.

How sure I am: very sure about the name and the layer. The bold vs. light text plus the layers icon made it clear which name belongs to which. 02 was the harder test, because "t3 code 1" and "t3 code" look almost the same, and I still wasn't confused. One small thing: I was slightly unsure whether the icon was just a label or something I could click, like a menu to change the layer. Nothing about it says it's a button, and I'm fine assuming it isn't.

## 2. The padlock (02 vs 03)
I'd expect it to lock the object so it can't be moved. 03 confirms that:
- The padlock changed from open to closed.
- The square resize handles and the round rotate knob are gone. Only a plain outline is left.
- A small padlock now appears next to "t3 code 1" in the Layers panel.
- The title changed to "Untitled •" and Undo became active, so locking counts as an edit I can undo.

The missing handles told me it was locked more clearly than the padlock did. At real size, an open padlock and a closed one look almost the same. The lighter box behind it in 03 is the hover effect, as you noted. I can't tell from these screenshots how obvious the locked padlock is once the mouse moves away.

## 3. The tip in 01
"Tip: Ctrl+L locks it in place, so you can draw over it without moving it." It's clear: press Ctrl+L to pin the picture you just added. It shows up at the right moment, just after you add a picture, which is exactly when you'd want to draw on it.

Would I remember it? Maybe once. It vanishes after a few seconds and doesn't mention the padlock on the label, so I wouldn't connect the two. What would really teach me is seeing "Ctrl+L" again on the padlock's hover tooltip. I couldn't check whether that tooltip exists, because no screenshot shows it.

## 4. Readable at real size?
Yes. In all the full screenshots I could read the label's name, layer and padlock without zooming: white on solid blue, small but clear. The tip at the bottom is easy to read.

## 5. What works, and is worth keeping
- Bold name / light layer name / layers icon. This is what makes "object vs layer" obvious, even in the "t3 code 1 / t3 code" case.
- Every part of the label matches the Layers panel and the properties panel exactly, so I trusted it straight away.
- With two objects selected (05) the label simply says "2 objects" + padlock. With text selected (06) it works the same way. It's consistent.
- The tip's timing and short wording.
- When locked, the handles disappear and a padlock appears in the Layers list. That's a strong, clear "you can't move this now".

## 6. Actually broken or confusing (only what I'm sure of)
- **In 01 the tip overlaps the zoom bar.** The dark message box covers the right-hand corner of the zoom/size box at the bottom left. The "1254 × 1254" text only just escapes being covered. It's cosmetic, but it's a real collision.

Nothing else.

## 7. The one thing I'd change
Make the lock state easier to see on the label itself. For example, give the closed padlock a filled or different-coloured background even when the mouse isn't over it, and show "Lock (Ctrl+L)" when you hover over it. That one change ties the tip and the button together, which is what the owner wants people to learn.

## Too big or too busy?
**It's fine.** It's one short line, about the height of a menu item. In 04 it does cover the top edge of the "CORNER BAKERY" sign, but only while something is selected, and it sits just above the selected object, which is where I'd expect it. I wouldn't add anything more to it.
