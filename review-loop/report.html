What I need you to decide
  1. Text without a backdrop: what is "the text area"? Your rule B4 says a border goes "around the backdrop, or around the text area if there is no backdrop". I built it as the whole text box. A box made by clicking is 320 px wide, so the border, and any arrow from it, sits up to about 90 px past the last letter. The auditor reads "text area" as the words themselves. I wrote that wording, so it's your call.
    My recommendation: the words. Without a backdrop, the border and arrow ends go round a box drawn tight round the lines of text. With a backdrop, nothing changes.
  2. Merging puts the result on top. Merge a screenshot and its label, but not the arrow between them, and the merged picture covers the arrow. This was already how merge worked; arrows just make it more noticeable.
    My recommendation: the merged picture takes the place of the highest merged part, not the very top of the layer. Anything that was above the merged parts stays above.

Glossary

Connector
An arrow with an end riding the edge of a text box, shape or marker. Dragging its middle bends it.
Mark on a picture
An arrow whose attached ends are all pinned to spots on pictures. It moves like any arrow.
Pin
An arrow end fixed to one spot of a picture, following it when the picture moves, turns or flips.
Serious / medium / low
Serious: a wrong picture or lost work nobody would notice. Medium: rare, or wrong but visible and undoable. Low: an annoyance or an odd edge case.
