Selection tag and Ctrl+L tip: review, round 1

This review covered readability, design coherence and clarity. It did not judge whether the features are worth having; that's your call. Three reviewers looked at screenshots: a newcomer, a readability specialist and a design specialist, on three different models. An auditor then checked how I rated their findings.

What's at stake: you, and anyone you show the app to. If it fails, someone is confused for a moment; no work is lost. The review stops after this one round, as you asked.

The tag, before and after

What the reviewers said

worked All three told the object's name from the layer's name at once, even with “t3 code 1” on the “t3 code” layer. All three found it readable at real size, and none found it too big or busy.
LOW · fixed The layer name was too faint. Its contrast was 3.7 to 1 against the blue, below the 4.5 to 1 recommended for small text. It's now full white.
LOW · filed, not fixed The tip runs into the zoom bar. This bug was already there, with other long messages too. Moving every message up made them cover the paste chooser, so I undid that and filed it in BUGS.md. Question 6 below asks whether to fix it.
needs you The open and closed padlock look alike at real size. This was the newcomer's one wish. The handles do vanish when an object is locked, but the tag itself only changes from an outline lock to a filled one. Right after you click it, the hover shade also hides the divider.
needs you The tip: it doesn't mention the padlock, so people won't link the two. It's gone after six seconds. Nothing else in the app starts with “Tip:”.
Smaller points (one line each)
  • The bigger tag covers more of the picture, for example the top of the bakery sign in the sample.
  • The tag has tighter corners than the app's pill-shaped floating bars. The designer reviewer noted that tight corners read as “attached to the selection”.
  • The Layers panel doesn't use the layers icon. The designer reviewer suggested also putting a type icon (picture, text) before the object's name.
  • Opening pictures with Open… doesn't show the tip, because nothing is selected afterwards.
  • The newcomer briefly wondered whether the layers icon is clickable.

Your decisions

  1. Tip wording. I recommend “Lock it in place with Ctrl+L or the padlock on its label, so you can draw over it without moving it”. It drops “Tip:” and links the shortcut to the padlock.
  2. Locked padlock. I recommend making it stand out: a white rounded square behind a blue lock whenever the object is locked, so the state shows without hovering.
  3. Tag size against covering the picture. I recommend keeping the new size, which every reviewer found readable.
  4. A type icon before the object name. I recommend against it. It adds clutter, and readers already matched the tag to the panel.
  5. Opening pictures shows the tip? I recommend not. It needs something selected, and the next picture you add will show it.
  6. The old message/zoom-bar overlap. I recommend fixing it as its own small change: centre messages in the space to the right of the zoom bar.
Glossary
  • Tag: the small blue label above whatever is selected.
  • Contrast 4.5 to 1: the usual minimum difference between text and its background for small text to be easy to read.
  • Filed: written down in BUGS.md to fix later, because the bug was there before this change.
