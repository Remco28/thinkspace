1. **HIGH — Re-pinning detaches connectors from hidden targets, contrary to C11.** Scenario: attach a line end to a picture, hide the picture or its layer, then drag the line, nudge it, or align it. The plan holds that pin aside during a drag, then calls `attachTargetAt` at the new point; that lookup considers visible objects, so it cannot find the hidden picture and the end becomes free. When the picture is shown again, the line no longer follows it. I checked the plan’s hidden-target freeze rule and re-pin rule against `EditorController.objectsAt` in [controller.dart](/home/frank/Projects/Thinkspace/lib/editor/controller.dart), which skips hidden layers and objects. Re-pin needs an exception that preserves attachments to hidden targets.

2. **MEDIUM — A bordered cut-out gets a rectangular shadow, contrary to B10.** Scenario: give a cut-out a border and shadow. The plan fills the border’s outer rectangle for the shadow, so transparent areas inside the cut-out receive shadow too; B10 says a cut-out’s shadow follows its visible outline. This is visibly wrong but easy to fix by changing the shadow shape. I checked the planned shadow cases against image rendering in [render.dart](/home/frank/Projects/Thinkspace/lib/editor/render.dart) and the cut-out alpha hit-testing in [scene.dart](/home/frank/Projects/Thinkspace/lib/model/scene.dart).

What is sound: re-pinning visible picture pins is needed so moving a line does not make relink snap its ends back, and `anchorBend` is justified because the clipped curve’s `p1`, `p2`, and `bend` alone do not preserve the original anchor-chord curve across relink passes.
